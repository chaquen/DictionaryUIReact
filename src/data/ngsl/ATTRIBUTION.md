## Changes made

The files `NGSL_12_stats.csv` (frequency rank and adjusted frequency per million) and
`NGSL_12_with_English_definitions.xlsx` (easy-English definitions) were joined by word and
reduced to `[term, rank, perMillion, definition]` rows with `scripts/build_ngsl.py`.
